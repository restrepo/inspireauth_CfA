Counting rules

The same rules as the Latin American study (repository restrepo/inspireauth), applied to the 54 African countries.

  • Institutions of a country: INSPIRE-HEP institutions whose first address has the country's ISO code. Their papers with ten or fewer authors, each counted once.
  • Every author of those papers is a record per author and institution, with the INSPIRE profile; the country of a record is that of its institution.
  • A country's papers are the papers of its author records whose affiliation is one of the country's institutions; authors are the distinct INSPIRE BAIs among its records; an author is active with a paper from the last five years.
  • Citations are summed over the country's papers; the h-index is computed on them.
  • Per million inhabitants uses the latest World Bank population; R&D is the World Bank average since 1996, HDI the UNDP average 1990–2022.
  • Collaboration: share of the row country's papers that also belong to the column country.
  • Ranks and mobility: authors with a declared rank in a country and the distinct institution, rank and period of the ranked papers they published.
  • Gender: inferred from the first given name and the country of the author's first record with genderize-offline (World Gender Name Dictionary 2.0); known at probability 0.9 or more.

Run it yourself

The scripts are in the repository restrepo/inspireauth_CfA. They need Python and the genderize-offline package; the download from INSPIRE-HEP takes tens of minutes, the rest a few seconds.

pip install -r reproduce/requirements.txt
python build/build_inspire.py
python reproduce/reproduce.py data/inspire_Africa.json.gz results.json
python reproduce/tables.py results.json page_data.json
python reproduce/export_dashboard.py page_data.json dash/
python reproduce/standalone.py dash/ board.html
