HECAP Africa boards

Each board shows the indicators of high-energy physics, cosmology and astroparticle physics in the 54 African countries from one download of INSPIRE-HEP (repository restrepo/inspireauth_CfA), following the study of Latin America by Morales-Alvarado, Quevedo, Ramos-Hamud and Restrepo (its boards). A new board is built each time a release of the repository is published. Every board is a self-contained page that also works offline.
